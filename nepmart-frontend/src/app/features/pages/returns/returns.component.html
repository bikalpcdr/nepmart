returns & refunds

Easy returns, fair refunds

Not happy with an order? Most items can be returned within 7 days — fresh produce within 24 hours. Here's how it works.

1. Return Windows

  • Fresh produce & dairy: report within 24 hours of delivery if quality is unsatisfactory.
  • Non-perishable items: return within 7 days of delivery, unused and in original packaging.
  • Damaged or wrong items: report within 48 hours with a photo — we cover the return shipping.

2. How to Start a Return

  1. Go to your order (or Track Order) and note the order ID.
  2. Contact support with the order ID and reason for return.
  3. Our team schedules a pickup (Valley) or shares a courier return label (outside Valley).
  4. Once the vendor receives and inspects the item, your refund is processed.

3. Refund Timelines

  • eSewa / Khalti / wallet: usually instant, within 24 hours at most.
  • Bank transfer / Connect IPS: 3–5 business days to your original account.
  • Cash on delivery: refund via eSewa/Khalti or store credit, your choice.

4. Non-Returnable Items

  • Perishable goods outside the windows above
  • Opened personal-care or hygiene products
  • Made-to-order or custom items
  • Items damaged after delivery due to misuse

Questions about a specific order? Contact us — we resolve most returns within one business day.
