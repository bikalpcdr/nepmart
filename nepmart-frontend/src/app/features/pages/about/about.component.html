about us

Fresh from Nepal, delivered with care

Nepmart is a modern marketplace connecting local farmers, makers, and shops with homes across Nepal — quality products, fair prices, and delivery you can count on.

Our Story

Nepmart started with a simple observation: amazing products were being grown and made all over Nepal, but getting them to city households was slow, expensive, and unreliable. We built a marketplace that puts local producers first and gives shoppers the convenience they expect from modern e-commerce.

Today we work with vendors from the Terai's farms to Kathmandu's kitchens, couriering everything from morning vegetables to festive gift boxes — fresh, checked, and fairly priced.

Our Mission

To make quality Nepali products one click away for every household, while helping local businesses grow with fair commissions, transparent payouts, and real customers.

Our Vision

A Nepal where buying local is the easiest, most trusted choice — for everyday groceries and special occasions alike.
